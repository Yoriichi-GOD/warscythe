## SYSTEM 1: TWIN EXECUTION PILLARS

### OPERATIONS (The One-Off Wars)

An operation is your declaration of war against a specific obstacle.

Not a vague goal. Not a "someday" ambition. A *concrete, time-bound commitment*. The moment you create it, you've crossed a threshold. You're no longer thinking about it. You're doing it.

Operations live in **regions**—40 distinct worlds, each ruled by a dragon. To progress, you complete operations. Each operation unlocks artifacts. Five operations liberate a fairy from imprisonment. Then you face the dragon in a Boss Raid.

**The Threat Level System:**
- **LOW (1 day):** Something you can finish in focused hyperfocus. Email cleanup. Single workout. Small write-up.
- **MEDIUM (3 days):** A project spanning a week. A presentation. A feature. A mile-marker.
- **HIGH (7 days):** Something that demands sustained focus. A major deliverable. Competitive training. Real creative work.
- **LEGENDARY (14+ days):** Only for the hardest wars. Launching a project. Exams. Transformation. These become Boss Raids.

The app doesn't negotiate. You can't mark a legendary task complete in 5 days. The system respects your commitment because *you* said this matters. It holds you accountable to your own declaration.

**Why this works for ADHD brains:**
You don't need motivation. You need *stakes*. High stakes = hyperfocus activation. The app doesn't try to trick you into productivity. It creates conditions where your ADHD becomes your superpower.

### RITUALS (The Daily Religion)

Rituals are different from operations.

Operations are wars—discrete, time-bound, with endings. Rituals are your daily religion. They're the non-negotiable habits that build you. Miss one, and the entire chain breaks.

Gym. Writing. Learning. Meditation. Breathing. Whatever builds you.

The app tracks them with a **global streak counter**. Complete all rituals today? Streak +1. Miss one? Streak resets to zero.

**Why all-or-nothing?**
Because you understand that consistency is the superpower. Not intensity. Not brilliance. *Consistency*. The ability to show up, even when you don't feel like it. Even when it's cold. Even when it doesn't matter. That habit is worth more than any single day of peak performance.

The streak is psychological. It makes the invisible visible. That number climbing *feels* like proof you're building something. That number resetting *hurts*—which is the point. You'll work harder to keep it alive.
