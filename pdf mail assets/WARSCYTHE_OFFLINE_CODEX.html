Technical explanation

  1. Authentication resolves a session and loads profile/domain state with timeout protection.
  2. Mutations are applied to local Zustand state immediately and persisted locally.
  3. Completion queues an idempotent progression event before domain synchronization.
  4. The server deduplicates the event, recomputes authoritative statistics, and returns the accepted state.
  5. Per-domain merge rules reconcile operations, rituals, inventory, fitness, and settings.
  6. Other devices obtain the reconciled result during their next fetch or synchronization.

YC walkthrough

Warscythe saves the action locally first, so completion feels immediate. In the background, the server prevents duplicate progression, calculates authoritative XP and level, and makes the reconciled state available across devices.

YC Briefing Room

Problem

Traditional productivity apps often make completion feel disposable. Warscythe turns execution into persistent identity, consequence, and visible world progression.

Founder insight

Users began comparing artifact rarity and finding their own progression strategies. The product’s visual craft acts as a retention mechanism rather than decorative polish.

Technical proof

Local-first UX, server-authoritative progression, idempotent event handling, domain sync, payment integration, and a staged data-integrity fix.
