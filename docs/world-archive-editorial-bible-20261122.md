# The Unquiet Post — editorial continuity

Implemented first-volume direction, 22 November 2026. This supersedes the region-per-five-chapters presentation. Earlier planning files remain historical proposals. The runtime manuscript is `src/features/archive/data/volumeOne.json`; its stable discovery IDs are the editorial identifiers below.

## The world and the limit on its power

All provinces inhabit one landscape on the back of one immense living dragon traveling through an astral cosmos. The divine Emperor and Empress are the parents of the carrier and its hostile sibling. The sibling makes the attacking dragons. Existing regional Empresses are distinct from the divine parent; no relationship to mortal Queen Nerys or Princess Maelin is asserted. Fitness deities receive no new genealogy.

The carrier deliberately keeps its inhabitants steady. It cannot simply roll to remove invaders without killing the people it shelters. It is not made helpless by forgetting its powers. Its care imposes a real constraint on how it can use them.

This is author knowledge. The reader initially receives attributed accounts and observable consequences. A civilian may deny a creature beneath the land while having personally seen an attacking dragon. Nobody must deny an obvious burned roof for the faith dispute to function.

Selen believes but submits his measurements to criticism. Vara doubts but revises a conclusion when evidence changes. Mara disputes the observatory's charges while feeding its observers. Faith and skepticism do not divide automatically into virtuous and foolish factions. Prayers are not asserted to operate the carrier like steering controls.

## One connected economy

The Threshold supplies markets, grain and courier exchanges; Bonehollow supplies ore and old workings; Ashenveil works the metal. Frostmere keeps preserved stores, an observatory and an infirmary. Shadowfen carries river and causeway traffic. The Oasis supplies water and caravan staging. Aurelius Keep houses court, treasury and mint. Stormspire maintains signals. The Abyss houses deep surveys and a medical station. Titan's Rest preserves old charters and bronze work.

These are narrative dependencies, not a simulated trading game or changes to app coin prices. Fictional silver marks are not Warscythe account coins.

The existing twenty-stage map labels and ten art-province names are not silently renamed by this delivery. The letters use the established art locales as places. A full gazetteer and map-stage reconciliation remains an editorial decision before new geographic art is commissioned.

## Calendar and material provenance

Civil year 642 is the working date of the first volume. Longrain has thirty days and is followed by Palesun. T0 is 21 Longrain, the Gannet evacuation. Earlier dates such as the copied charter's 618 establish age without inventing a complete calendar. Harvest in the burial letter refers to the earlier season; no current mine operation is implied.

Dates describe writing, not discovery. This is a collected edition of originals and attributed copies. Nonchronological discovery does not teleport an unsent original between people. Corren's last letter remains in his case until recovery on 7 Palesun (+16), reaches the Keep on 9 Palesun (+18), and can then be encountered as a retained paper or copy. Maelin's correction is dated 10 Palesun (+19).

### Currency and gate responsibility

Seven of Tavin's twelve suspect coins were struck light, five clipped. Deren privately signs the debasement decision expecting ore receipts to resume. Edda's burial returns and Mere's freight duplicate contradict the receipts. The hearing establishes responsibility but cannot produce silver the treasury does not have. Grain later pays part of the debt; it does not erase every claimant's loss.

Deren also signs the chain diversion. Defending the capital is a real problem, but concealing the change and relying on an outdated survey have consequences. He is not secretly responsible for every attack or every failed signal. No unearned villain confession resolves the entire plot.

### Observations and the maker

The three stations record a shared displacement without a corresponding tilt. Vara notes that this alone does not distinguish a moving sky, moving land or a creature. Hest later exposes a warm continuous seam without taking a damaging sample. Edda's cold attacker fragment has repeated edge hollows and different thermal behavior. Hest refuses to collapse both objects into one conclusion because both were called scales.

An old primer, bronze charter and concealed leaf preserve versions of the divine family account. Pell recognizes counting marks from old court bindings; their appearance under the attacker plates supports manufacture, not an instant command word. Author truth about the hostile sibling remains fixed, while the characters' route to knowing it is incomplete. Pell's release and the definitive identification of the maker are deliberate future story obligations, not claims that the first volume has resolved them.

## Editorial closure and next expansion gate

This delivery is one authored 48-document volume, not franchise-scale worldbuilding. Its unresolved obligations are explicit: Pell's safety, the surviving lower prisoners, restitution, a shared gazetteer and how the maker can be identified without injuring the carrier. Write and review the next volume before raising the runtime catalogue cap. Do not ship procedural regional filler to imply unlimited authored depth.

Before adding new lore, check the fixed author truth, writing/recovery/delivery dates, who has actually seen each item, the cost of transporting evidence and the effect on an existing ordinary life. Contradictions must have a traceable cause. “Unreliable narrator” is not an eraser for accidental continuity errors.
