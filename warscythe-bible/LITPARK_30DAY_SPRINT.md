## ALGORITHM RULEBOOK

**Format:** Every post is a Reel. No static posts. Reels get 3-5x the reach.

**Hook:** First 1-2 seconds must stop the scroll. No slow intros. No text-only openers. Movement or bold text overlay from frame one.

**Audio:** Use trending audio on every post. Check Instagram's trending audio weekly. Even a 5-second clip on a trending sound gets algorithm push. Mute the original audio if needed, layer the trend on top.

**Captions:** One punchy line. No paragraphs. The reel speaks. Caption echoes, doesn't explain.

**Hashtags:** 3-5 only. Not 30. Not 0. Targeted:
- Warscythe posts: `#habittracker #productivityapp #darkfantasy`
- Book posts: `#poetry #indiepoet #BGMIIndia`
- Meme posts: `#PeterGriffin #memes #IndianMemes`

**Engagement window:** Reply to every comment within the first 60 minutes of posting. Instagram uses early engagement velocity to decide distribution.

**No links in captions.** Bio only. Keeps you out of the suppression bucket.

**Cross-post to X:** Every Reel goes to X as well. Different caption. X gets the sharper, drier take. Instagram gets the visual-first hook.

## WEEK-BY-WEEK STRATEGY

**Week 1 (Days 1-7): Establish the voice**
Ship everything. No overthinking. Shyness breaks on Day 1 and never comes back. First cinematic edit on Day 7 is the first post you actually spend time on. Everything before it is volume.

**Week 2 (Days 8-14): Find the signal**
By Day 10 you'll know which pillar is pulling reach. Don't change the rotation yet. Let data accumulate. Second cinematic edit on Day 14 is the quality benchmark.

**Week 3 (Days 15-21): Double down**
Check which posts have the highest saves/shares. Whatever that pillar is — increase its frequency slightly. If Peter Griffin is running at 10x the reach of book content, Abhay does 2 Peter Griffin edits on some days. If book content is converting, push those harder.

**Week 4 (Days 22-30): Close strong**
You're building toward YC submission on July 25. Final cinematic edit on Day 28 is the most polished thing you produce. It becomes the content you show YC. Last 2 days: post the two or three best-performing formats from the sprint. End on your highest-confidence content, not your newest.
