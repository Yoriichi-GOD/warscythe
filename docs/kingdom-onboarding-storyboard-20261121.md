# Warscythe kingdom lore and onboarding storyboard

The kingdom lives on the back of a benevolent dragon, a child of the divine Emperor and Empress. Its sibling creates the hostile dragons that occupy the regions. The player restores passage through useful work, while the regional Empresses preserve the places and histories being restored.

This is the proposed narrative connecting the user's canon to the existing app. **Worldbearer** and **Betrayer** are descriptive titles, not newly approved character names. The divine parents remain separate from the existing regional Empresses. The current regional names, portraits, trophies, individual chronicles and Fitness deity system are retained.

## The player’s introduction

The first screen still offers one useful action: create an Operation. Practice teaches creating, inspecting and validating an Operation. Practice does not advance the ten real Operations that open the campaign. Each milestone then introduces one concept through a short Guardian exchange and the existing interactive lesson where applicable.

The roadmap button opens a Kingdom Chronicle with readable chapters and a separate Unlock map view. Chapter 1 supplies the initial premise. Further story chapters open at their existing milestones; later victories are not exposed early. The next action is shown separately from the currently selected story chapter. Reading a chapter never awards progress or bypasses a gate.

Fitness has its own optional-entry practice path: choose a split, add an exercise and set, enter load and reps, complete the set, inspect recovery, adjust RPE, and finish the rehearsal. Its report stays out of the real reward ceremony. Real workout logging preserves the existing reward contract.

### 1. The Living Kingdom

The ground beneath your feet rises and falls like breath. It is breath. Every road, forest and citadel of this kingdom rests upon a living dragon: the Worldbearer, child of the divine Emperor and Empress. Something has begun to wound that ancient shelter. The Guardian places a dormant Scythe in your hands. Begin with one promise you can keep.

**Player action:** Complete one real Operation. The Scythe and its command controls will open.

**PNG brief:** A vast, benevolent dragon carries the inhabited regions along its back; dawn, tiny settlements, no lettering. Separate transparent Guardian and dormant Scythe foregrounds.

**Transition:** Fade from one breathing scale to the kingdom silhouette. Use an opacity/transform transition of about 160–240 ms for the live UI. Future cinematic movement should pause when offscreen and collapse to an immediate state change with reduced motion. The reading text remains still once it appears.

## How this relates to the existing regional lore

The regional chronicles remain local witness accounts. Their dragons can still be described through fire, famine, storms or grief; the new overarching account identifies the sibling as the source behind those forms. Regional Empresses remain the keepers the player frees, not additional names for the divine mother. The Worldbearer is never an enemy or a kill target. Region liberation must show an invader falling while the inhabited ground remains alive.

Some existing labels use a UI region name such as The Threshold alongside an Ashwood Empress or soundscape identifier. This pass preserves those references. A later lore editorial pass should establish whether these are provinces, older names or asset identifiers before renaming any established locations. No existing chapter is silently rewritten to make the new myth fit.

## Why teach it this way

Bandai Namco’s [Elden Ring early-game guide](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/news/elden-ring-early-game-tips) describes a practice area, teaching that depends on the player's build, and tutorial text that can be reread. Bethesda’s [Skyrim manual](https://assets.ctfassets.net/rporu91m20dc/1poliwondakI2QwKQMY2QI/b79ecee4541dd1ba7c7a85b453f0e9b2/manual_skyrim-le_pc_en-us.pdf) describes choosing active quests in the journal, finding their targets, and returning to help. The design inference for Warscythe is to pair a useful action with its context, keep the next objective clear, and let players reread the world's explanation. The complexity can remain discoverable without putting every system into the first conversation.

The workout logger draws on a narrower reference: [Hevy’s per-set RPE and previous-values workflow](https://help.hevyapp.com/hc/en-us/articles/34490600233111-RPE-vs-RIR-What-They-Mean-and-How-to-Use-Them-in-Hevy). Warscythe now shows previous recorded values beside a set, keeps load/reps/type/RPE/completion together, and puts notes behind a disclosure. The app retains its current strength-workout model; cardio duration, distance, GPS, coaching and new reward formulas are outside this change.

## Phone recommendations after this pass

Implemented: collapsible scythe and main deity artwork, readable set rows, 44px workout controls, a focused workout screen, sticky minimize/rest controls and an always-available resume action when a workout is active. Affinity recognition remains visible when the decorative deity display is collapsed.

Recommended next: make the Operations and Rituals columns a single stack below tablet width, with the collapsed scythe summary above a fully readable task list. The current two-column phone composition still truncates long task titles. Treat this as a separate layout decision for review, since the requested preservation of the overall UI argues against silently replacing it here. Keep advanced metadata inside each task’s detail view and retain all parameters. Revisit the mandatory Shop walkthrough separately: its purchase-free inspection is preserved, but making that lesson optional could shorten the campaign interruption without hiding the Shop itself.
