# The Chain That Never Arrived

Pilot document cycle for Warscythe. Editorial draft, not implemented canon. New personal names, the Gannet Causeway, requisition numbers, monetary amounts and relative dates are provisional. Existing provinces/Empresses retain their names; mortal court relationships still need approval. Each item can be read alone. Read in a different order to test whether its meaning changes without its facts changing.

## 01 — An alteration to a smith's estimate

**Origin:** Iven's forge, The Threshold. **Written:** day -18. **Found:** folded around an unfinished spearhead in a market storeroom. **Connects:** Ashenveil's missing iron, the shared currency and later relief requisition. This paper is not addressed to the player.

Joric—

The estimate was eight silver marks when I made it. It is twenty-three now. No, that is not for a pair.

Ashenveil has sent neither iron nor word in seventeen days. The merchant wants payment before he will tell me whether his next wagon contains metal. The charcoal man has discovered that fear burns as well as wood, and charges accordingly.

Your deposit still stands. If you cannot afford the spear, bring your father's. I can set a new socket around the old head.

Tell your boy to stop striking stones with it. I cannot forge him a second inheritance.

Iven

*A later pencil addition, beneath the price: Ask the sages whether the dragon eats charcoal. Perhaps we have been bidding against it.*

## 02 — Queen Nerys to the treasury at Aurelius Keep

**Origin:** the Oasis court's temporary quarters at the western cistern. **Written:** day -14. **Found:** a retained diplomatic copy. **Connects:** the Keep's finances, refugees and the difference between a reassuring stamp and actual material. Nerys is a proposed mortal queen, not a renamed Empress Amara.

To the Keeper of the Treasury,

Your last payment bore the correct face and the wrong weight. I have enclosed three of the coins. A portrait cannot make up the missing silver.

We have opened the western cistern to your refugees. We will keep it open. But a bucket must be made somewhere, a mule must eat something, and the men repairing the sluice will not take royal assurances home for supper.

Send grain, sound metal, or an answer that can be weighed.

My chaplain says a stranger bearing a scythe will come to free us. I have given him a room and a place at my table. Until the stranger arrives, I require the grain.

Nerys

*A clerk's endorsement: The enclosed coins were returned to the general chest. No reply was entered.*

## 03 — Princess Maelin's promise

**Origin:** Aurelius Keep. **Written:** day -12. **Recipient:** Ser Corren at Gannet Causeway. **Found:** in the captain's dispatch case, stained at one corner. **Connects:** requisition 27; the blue cord on the original letter; the later correction. No relationship to Empress Auren is asserted.

Captain,

Requisition twenty-seven is signed: three hundred lengths of chain, two supply carts, and leave for both carts to return with civilians. The chain is for the crossing. Do not permit another office to borrow it along the way.

The cord binding this letter carries my office. Show it where your orders are questioned.

You asked whether you should continue to hold the eastern bank. Hold it while it serves the people crossing. When it begins to serve only the map, leave it.

I have put that plainly so no one may later tell you I meant otherwise.

Maelin

*Under the signature, in the same hand: I remember the bridge at summer fair. I have not forgotten that there is a village beside it.*

## 04 — A treasury instruction, not intended for circulation

**Origin:** Minister Deren's office. **Written:** day -10. **Recipient:** the western works quartermaster. **Found:** a duplicate concealed inside an account binding. **Connects:** the exact cargo from document 03 and its altered destination.

The western hinges have split in three places. The engineer's estimate is two nights under attack, possibly one.

Transfer the chain allocated under requisition 27 to the gate works. Record the movement under continuation of provincial relief. The capital's defenses are provincial relief; if the western gate falls, no further convoy leaves this city.

Gannet can be braced in timber until another shipment arrives. Consult the previous season's survey. Do not requisition another survey now; we need the men carrying wood.

Send the Princess a duplicate after delivery. An argument while the carts stand loaded will mend neither gate nor crossing.

Deren

*The survey attached to the duplicate predates the flood season.*

## 05 — A dispatch ledger, with one destination overwritten

**Origin:** the Gannet road office. **Written:** day -7. **Found:** a torn leaf used to wrap a bottle. **Connects:** the royal promise to the diversion. This is evidence, not an omniscient explanation.

**27 — Provincial relief**

Three hundred lengths, iron chain. Two carts. Received under the Princess's seal.

Destination: ~~Gannet crossing~~ Western gate works, Aurelius Keep.

Authority for alteration: treasury duplicate, witnessed at departure.

Message to Captain Corren: to follow by separate rider.

*In the narrow margin: No rider entered the muster. I was told to leave a space for the name.*

*Initial: N.*

## 06 — The letter Corren could not send

**Origin:** Gannet Causeway. **Written:** day -1. **Recovered:** from the captain's dispatch case on day +16, delivered to the Keep on day +18. **Found by the player later:** in Maelin's archive with the private reply, or as an explicitly attributed copy. **Connects:** documents 03, 05, 07 and 09. Author-level outcome: Corren dies during the evacuation on day 0; the reader does not need to know that before reading this.

Your Highness,

No chain. No message from the rider.

We have taken the hinges from every empty house on the bank. The carpenter says he can give us one more crossing before the water takes the frame. He will not give me a second estimate.

I have tied the blue cord from your letter to the last cart. There are no crests left here that any gatekeeper is likely to recognize. They may still recognize yours.

The people have stopped asking when the supplies will come. They ask which children go first.

I am sending the cart before the answer becomes a quarrel.

Corren

*A second paragraph begins on the reverse and stops after: My mother's levy is entered under the old house, although she has not lived there since—*

## 07 — Extract from the court's casualty register

**Origin:** Aurelius Keep. **Written:** day +2. **Found:** a public register in a requisition hall. **Connects:** contradicts the meaning of Corren's withdrawal, while leaving a trail to the private papers.

Gannet Causeway: position lost.

Stores: unrecovered. Crown transport: one cart absent from allocation.

Captain Corren: unaccounted for following abandonment of the eastern post.

Persons carried from the post: numbers unconfirmed. No commissioned officer accompanied them.

Assessment of surviving property and transport losses to follow.

*A petitioner has pressed a dirty thumb beside the word abandonment. Beneath it, in another hand: There were people in that cart.*

## 08 — A prison scrap sewn into a laundry hem

**Origin:** a holding cell under the occupied Shadowfen road station. **Written:** day +11. **Intended recipient:** Maelin, through anyone still traveling toward the Keep. **Found:** with the folded ledger leaf from document 05. **Connects:** confirms that the absent rider was a documented gap, not proof that Corren received a cancellation.

For the woman whose seal is tied in blue.

I kept the first destination. They told me to scrape it clean. I ruled one line through it instead.

Twenty-seven. Three hundred lengths. The gates of the Keep.

There was no rider. Not late. None. I left the space because I was ordered to leave it.

They call Corren a man who ran. I watched the iron go the other way.

If you can get this out, do not send anyone back for me by the main stairs. The guards have moved the prisoners below the road. Find the laundry door.

Neral, way-clerk

*The final fold has been rubbed almost transparent. The numerals remain legible.*

## 09 — Maelin's correction, retained with an unsent reply

**Origin:** Aurelius Keep. **Written:** day +19. **Found:** the corrected register and the private draft together. **Connects:** completes the paper trail without pretending the record can undo the death. Corren's death has now been corroborated by survivors.

**For the register:**

Strike abandonment. Enter: withdrawal of civilians under a written order of my office.

Enter Captain Corren among those killed at the crossing. Enter the civilians by name. If the names do not fit the column, widen the column.

The missing cart was not missing.

Maelin

**A private sheet, not dispatched:**

Captain,

Your cart reached the western gate. I saw the cord and thought the supplies had reached you.

I have been ashamed of many things since. Most of all, how relieved I was to recognize my own knot.

I have found your mother. Her levy has been removed from the old house.

I do not know why I am telling you this as though a reply is still possible.

## 10 — From Iven's daybook

**Origin:** The Threshold. **Written:** later in the same season. **Found:** between orders for repaired hinges and farm tools. **Connects:** the opening price dispute to continuing ordinary life and the faith argument. A conversation exists here because the smith recorded it.

Selen's novice came for three bell-clappers. Asked why they cost so much.

I told him the road was closed.

He said the carrier would provide.

I said I would be grateful if it provided a wagon.

He laughed. Paid for two. Asked me to hold the third until next week.

Worth remembering: the sages may not all be rich.

*Below, an unrelated order: Two hinges for the widow at North Lane. Charge the court account. Ask whether that account is real this time.*

## 11 — Sage Selen to a skeptical surveyor

**Origin:** Frostmere's observatory. **Recipient:** Surveyor Vara at Stormspire. **Written:** date to be fixed within the common calendar. **Found:** with observational tables, not as a public sermon. **Connects:** two distant regions observing the same sky; keeps the disagreement intellectually open.

Vara,

Your objection is fair. A misaligned instrument proves only that someone should repair the instrument.

We reset the sighting line. The displacement remained. The water gauge and hanging weight showed no corresponding tilt. Your own figures record the same change at the same watch. The Abyss station's reply agrees on the direction, though I do not trust their clock.

That is an observation. It is not yet a creature.

I believe the old account. You know that. I am sending you the measurements because belief does not excuse me from sending them.

If your explanation survives the three records, I will enter it beside mine.

Selen

## 12 — A private prayer on the back of a fuel account

**Origin:** attributed provisionally to Selen; handwriting comparison is a later editorial/discovery decision. **Found:** behind the observatory's winter accounts. **Connects:** the still ground, the disagreement and the carrier's protective constraint. It expresses faith; the document alone does not certify the cosmology.

They ask why you do not turn away from the things hunting us.

I have asked it too.

Today I watched a child carry a bowl across the infirmary without spilling it. I thought of the fields, the sick, the towers, every small flame still standing upright.

I do not ask you to turn.

Hold as long as you can.

Forgive us if we have mistaken your gentleness for your absence.

## Editorial connection check

27 is the same requisition in documents 03–05 and 08. Three hundred lengths never reach the crossing. The blue cord begins on Maelin's letter, is tied to the last refugee cart and is recognized at the Keep. Corren's letter is unsent, recovered on day +16 and delivered on day +18, so Maelin cannot answer its words before then. Her day +19 correction also relies on survivors and the leaked paperwork; it does not assume she read an unsent note on day +2. The private reply is found with the later corrected record, after she has learned more.

The fuel account is not evidence that the sages pilot the dragon. Selen's observations do not conclusively prove the carrier. Nerys's mention of a liberator records a chaplain's belief, not a universal prediction all citizens must share. Document 10 is a moment of ordinary life, not a key to every mystery.

Before publication, fix the common calendar, confirm mortal royal offices and place the proposed crossing on the approved regional map. This pilot deliberately leaves those decisions visible rather than silently replacing existing geography.
