Place your generated assets for the Kailash (Shiva) theme in this directory.

- `scythe-dormant.png` - The theme scythe at Dormant level.
- `scythe-awakened.png` - The theme scythe at Awakened level.
- `scythe-hardened.png` - The theme scythe at Hardened level.
- `scythe-refined.png` - The theme scythe at Refined level.
- `scythe-ascended.png` - The theme scythe at Ascended level.
- `scythe-platinum.png` - The theme scythe at Platinum level.
