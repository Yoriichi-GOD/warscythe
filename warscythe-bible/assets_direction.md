# Warscythe V4 — Asset Design & Art Direction Guide

This guide details the visual and auditory specifications for the V4 release. The goal is to maintain the **"Elden Ring 4K" Dark Fantasy** aesthetic: raw, high-contrast, heavily textured, atmospheric, and highly polished.

---

## 1. Core Visual Style & Rendering Standards

* **Aesthetic**: Grim dark fantasy, high-detail chiaroscuro lighting, deep shadows, and cinematic rim glows.
* **Materials**: Heavy metals (obsidian, hammered iron, scorched bronze, weathered steel), organic bones, glowing runes, and crackling elemental energy.
* **Format**: All weapon sprites should be transparent PNGs, formatted at high resolution (1024x1024 minimum, with crisp downscaling to 512x512).
* **Lighting**: Avoid flat colors or simple gradients. Use dramatic source lighting, specular highlights on metal edges, and atmospheric fog or particle textures embedded directly in the asset.

## 2. Premium Paid Scythe Evolution Tracks (6 Stages per Scythe)

Unlike standard progression weapons, each **Paid Premium Scythe** is a complete evolution pack containing **6 distinct images** matching the evolution stages from `DORMANT` to `PLATINUM`. The progression must feel visually rewarding:
* **DORMANT**: Crude, chipped, dark, inactive. No light or glow. Thick, heavy, unrefined silhouette.
* **AWAKENED**: Cracks in the blade begin to leak faint energy. Metal is slightly cleaned.
* **HARDENED**: Heavy physical reinforcements (spikes, grips, rivets) are added to the shaft and blade support.
* **REFINED**: The weapon is razor-sharp. Runes become legible, glowing with steady, concentrated light.
* **ASCENDED**: Energy flares outwards. High specular metal sheen. Shifting ambient aura.
* **PLATINUM**: The ultimate form. Gilded filigree, intense crackling energy, floaty runic elements, and legendary size.

## 5. UI Scroll Paper & Lore Atlas Direction

To capture the physical sensation of uncovering lost records, the Lore Scroll must look tactile and authentic.

### A. Scroll Texture (`scroll-paper.png`)
* **Color**: Warm sepia, faded tan, weathered parchment.
* **Details**: Frayed, slightly burnt edges. Faint watermark of the Warscythe emblem in the background. Smudges, oil spots, and ink blots on the corners.
* **Ratio**: A tall, vertical aspect ratio matching the screen height of mobile and desktop containers.

### B. Scroll Rollers (`scroll-roller-top.png`, `scroll-roller-bottom.png`)
* Horizontal wood cylindrical rollers wrapped with brass end-caps.
* Anchored at the top and bottom of the scroll container to give a three-dimensional depth, making the text appear to sit inside a rolled-out scroll.

### C. Lore Text & Content Direction
* **Topic**: Dark legends detailing the origins of the Warscythe, the fall of the Empress, the nature of the Voidwalkers, and the history of each of the 5 main regions.
* **Volume**: Short, poetic, high-impact paragraphs (max 100-150 words per page).
* **Tone**: Cryptic, ancient, and motivating. It serves as direct motivation for consistency: *your execution is what recovers history.*

## 6. Soundscape Jukebox Widget (`soundscape_jukebox.png`)

* **Core Concept**: A mystical, floating dark fantasy musical relic used as a physical UI control to toggle and play ambient region soundscapes.
* **Aesthetic**: Hammered obsidian body with faint brass gears, gold-engraved runes, and active glowing keyholes.
* **Behavior & States**:
  * **On Boot / Floating**: Stays anchored on the bottom-left corner of the viewport, gently floating up and down using micro-animations.
  * **Playing / Active**: Surrounded by a glowing, pulsing golden runic aura (`glow-pulse`).
  * **Paused / Inactive**: The glowing aura fades out completely, and it sits in a dormant, non-illuminated state.
