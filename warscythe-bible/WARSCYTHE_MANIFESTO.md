# ⚔️ WARSCYTHE: The Ultimate Gamified Productivity Engine

**Warscythe** is not a to-do list. It is a psychological engine designed to turn discipline into a visceral, addictive, and highly rewarding dark-fantasy experience. By blending high-fidelity aesthetics, progressive gamification, and ruthless momentum mechanics, Warscythe ensures that users don't just "check off tasks"—they conquer them.

---

## 🏛️ 1. The Twin Pillars of Execution

Warscythe divides human effort into two distinct categories to prevent mental fatigue and promote clear strategic focus:

### A. Operations (One-off Tasks)
* **Location:** The Operations Tab (Left Panel).
* **Meaning:** These are targeted, single-execution strikes. Things that have a start, an end, and a specific deadline (e.g., "Finish Q3 Report", "Pay Taxes").
* **Mechanics:** 
  * Capped at **3 Active Operations** at any given time. This forces ruthless prioritization and prevents the cognitive overwhelm of an endless to-do list.
  * **Recalculate Protocol:** A built-in "Break it Down" feature that theoretically slices overwhelming tasks into micro-steps to reduce friction.

### B. Rituals (Habits)
* **Location:** The Rituals Tab.
* **Meaning:** These are the foundational habits that shape identity. Things meant to be repeated daily or weekly (e.g., "1 Hour Deep Work", "Morning Workout").
* **Mechanics:**
  * **Streak Tracking:** Each ritual tracks its own continuous completion streak.
  * **Decay Mechanics:** If a daily ritual is missed, its individual streak resets to zero, emphasizing the importance of unbroken momentum.

---

## 🗡️ 2. The Weapon Evolution System (XP Progression)

The core visual anchor of the application is the **Reaper's Scythe**. It is a direct reflection of the user's accumulated effort (Total XP). As the user conquers tasks, the Scythe evolves.

* **DORMANT (0-49 XP):** The scythe sleeps, its edge dull. It awaits the first spark of will.
* **AWAKENED (50-149 XP):** A wooden frame bound with determination. The journey begins.
* **HARDENED (150-299 XP):** Cold iron forged through repeated action. A reliable tool of war.
* **REFINED (300-599 XP):** Silver-edged and balanced. Precision becomes your greatest weapon.
* **ASCENDED (600+ XP):** Golden resonance. Your actions now ripple through the fabric of reality.
* **PLATINUM (The Masterpiece):** A blade of pure focus. Distraction cannot touch this edge.

---

## 🔥 3. The Streak-Based Identity System (Ultimate Artifacts)

While XP tracks total effort over time, the **Streak System** tracks *consistency*. This is the most brutal and rewarding mechanic in Warscythe. 

By logging in and completing actions consecutively, the user climbs through identity tiers. Achieving these tiers visually upgrades the Scythe into its **Ultimate/Cosmic variants**:

1. **NEOPHYTE (5 Days):** The awakening of discipline.
2. **ACOLYTE (15 Days):** The commitment hardens.
3. **REAPER (30 Days):** A force of nature is born.
4. **EXECUTIONER (60 Days):** Flawless, emotionless execution.
5. **SOVEREIGN (120 Days):** Mastery over oneself and one's domain.
6. **VOID-WALKER (200 Days):** Moving effortlessly through reality.
7. **ETERNAL (300 Days):** Discipline that outlasts time itself.
8. **DEATH-LORD (360 Days):** The final evolution. Unstoppable. Unbreakable.

> [!WARNING] 
> **The Decay:** Missing a day resets the Global Streak back to 0. The Ultimate visual variants are stripped away, returning the user to their standard XP Scythe, providing immense psychological motivation to maintain the streak.

---

## 🗺️ 4. Tactical Map & Regional Conquest

Execution is mapped to a grand campaign. Every time a user completes **3 Operations or Rituals**, they conquer a "Region" on the Map.

* **Lore Unlocks:** Conquering a region unlocks a fragment of dark-fantasy lore. This gives narrative meaning to productivity. The lore serves as a metaphor for overcoming internal resistance, procrastination, and self-doubt.
* **Visual Progression:** The map visually updates, stamping conquered territories with a golden seal, providing long-term macro-progression alongside daily micro-progression.

---

## 💎 5. The Artifact Vault (Loot System)

To trigger the brain's dopamine response, Warscythe utilizes a variable-ratio reward system via Loot Drops. Upon completing an initiative, there is a chance to unearth an "Artifact".

### Resistance Levels (Effort):
When creating a task/ritual, users assign a difficulty, which dictates XP payout and Loot Rarity chances:
1. **RECON (Low Effort):** 10 base XP. Drops Common/Uncommon items.
2. **SKIRMISH (Medium Effort):** 20 base XP. Drops Uncommon/Rare items.
3. **ASSAULT (High Effort):** 40 base XP. High chance for Epic loot.
4. **BOSS RAID (Extreme Effort):** 100 base XP. The only way to hunt Legendary and Mythic artifacts.

### The Artifacts:
Artifacts are not just images; they are psychological totems. 
* *Common items* (e.g., Iron Quill) represent baseline execution.
* *Mythic items* (e.g., Crown of the Void) represent transcending human limits. 
All discovered items are permanently stored in the user's **Vault**.

---

## 📜 6. The Ledger (Historical Log)

The Ledger is the warlord's permanent record. It tracks every completed strike, the exact date, the XP earned, and the resistance level faced. It serves as undeniable proof of the user's hard work, vital for reflecting on past victories during moments of low motivation.

---

## ⚡ 7. Native Mobile & Immersive Triggers

Warscythe bridges the gap between web app and native mobile experience using CapacitorJS integrations:

* **Haptics:** Slicing through a task triggers physical device vibration. A Boss Raid triggers a `HEAVY` impact, while a standard Skirmish triggers a `MEDIUM` impact, making completion feel physically satisfying.
* **Offline Sanctum:** If the user loses internet connection, the app detects it instantly, throwing a premium toast notification: *"ENTERED OFFLINE SANCTUM. PROGRESS SAVED LOCALLY."* All operations can continue seamlessly offline.
* **Streak Crisis Alerts:** The app requests notification permissions and schedules local device alerts. If the user hasn't executed a ritual in a while, they receive a push notification: *"STREAK CRISIS: YOUR WEAPON'S EDGE IS DECAYING. CONQUER A RITUAL NOW."*

## 🎨 8. UI/UX & Design Philosophy

The interface is engineered to feel like a **Command Core**.
* **Palette:** Deep void blacks (`#050505`), tactical grays, and striking gold (`#c5a059`) for primary actions. Crimson red (`#ff3333`) is reserved strictly for destructive actions (deletions, warnings).
* **Typography:** Utilitarian, uppercase monospace fonts combined with elegant display fonts to mimic high-end military/fantasy HUDs.
* **Micro-interactions:**
  * The "Validating Execution" slash animation that physically cuts across the screen upon task completion.
  * The spinning Command Core compass in the navigation bar.
  * Glassmorphism panels that blur the background, adding depth and hierarchy to the information architecture.

### SUMMARY

Warscythe is the intersection of task management and hardcore RPG mechanics. It takes the mundane act of checking off a to-do list and elevates it into a daily battle for mastery, where every action builds your weapon, your identity, and your empire.
