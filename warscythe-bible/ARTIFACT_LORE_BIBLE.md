## 1. SKULL (Mythic Relic)

**Hook:** "Your hands are calloused. The friction is your language."

**Lore:**
The skull of the first enemy you defeated. Not a trophy of war—a **trophy of beginning**. Most people never start. You didn't just start; you bled doing it. Your hands still ache from the grip. That ache is data. It proves you fought when fighting seemed impossible. The skull watches over your arsenal as proof: **You are the type who starts.**

It makes you a specialist who said **no** to 99% to say **yes** to 1%. Your hyperfocus isn't scattered. It's *directed*. And that direction is sovereignty.

Each region assigns 5 artifacts from this 25-type pool:
- Example: Region 1 "The Threshold" might drop {Skull, Crown, Blade, Orb, Ring}
- Example: Region 6 "Aurelius Keep" might drop {Gem, Eye, Idol, Lantern, Scroll}
- Rotation ensures discovery across the game

While the core lore remains consistent, lore text can receive region-specific flavor:
- **Frozen regions** might reference "crystalline clarity" in Hook
- **Volcanic regions** might reference "forged in fire" in Lore
- **Shadow regions** might reference "clarity in darkness"

The core meaning stays intact; the metaphor adapts.

Same artifact can drop across rarities:
- **Common Crown** = First version (5-10 XP worth)
- **Mythic Crown** = Later version (100+ XP worth)

No mechanical difference. Pure visual intensity via drip effect.
